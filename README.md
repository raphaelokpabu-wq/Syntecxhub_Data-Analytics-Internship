📊 Internship Project 1 — Complete! 🎉

As part of my Data Analytics internship at Syntecx Hub, I designed a Power BI dashboard from a messy, inconsistent sales performance dataset — and honestly, I learned more from the messy parts than the pretty ones.

The goal: take raw, unstructured sales data and turn it into something that actually tells a story. Here's the stack that got me there:

🔹 Excel — cleaning inconsistent dates, stripping currency symbols out of price fields, and catching duplicate rows hiding in plain sight 🔹 Power Query — turning that mess into something structured and repeatable, instead of fixing it by hand every single time 🔹 Power Pivot / DAX — building measures like Total Revenue and Total Profit that calculate live from the data, instead of relying on static numbers 🔹 Power BI — bringing it all together into KPI cards, trend charts, and region/category breakdowns that actually tell a story, not just display numbers

The biggest takeaway? Data analysis isn't really about the dashboard. It's about everything that happens before it — the cleanup, the structuring, the "wait, why is this number wrong" moments that teach you more than any tutorial does.

Grateful for the opportunity to build this as part of my journey with Syntecx Hub. Really proud of how Project 1 turned out — on to the next one. 🚀
